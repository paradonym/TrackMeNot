General

Search Engines

Selection

To add the engine URL, search 'trackmenot' (without the quotes) on the engine you want to add, and copy/paste the search URL into the URL field below.

Avg. Query Rate

Activity presets control how frequently decoy searches are issued: Low — very few queries per day; Medium — a few per hour; High — frequent (roughly ~1/minute). The extension randomizes timing to avoid regular patterns.
